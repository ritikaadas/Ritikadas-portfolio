1

Audit and risk

EY, 46 months

HR audit and operational risk management. I learned to read an organization through its controls, and to notice where those controls quietly stop working.

Rigour is mostly the habit of asking what would have to be true for this to work.

2

The turn toward people

MBA, HR specialization. SHRM-CP and aPHR

I moved from controls to behaviour: why a well-designed process still fails when the people inside it experience it differently than the designer intended.

The design is only half the system. The other half is how it feels on a Tuesday.

3

Applied people work

Internships and live projects

Frontline attrition in a national sales force, capability building in a scaling retail brand, and organization development for wide scale expansion. Same question each time: what is actually happening here?

Data finds the pattern. Conversations find the reason. You need both before you recommend anything.

4

Writing the questions down

Chai, Culture & Career. Readably Yours

Two Substack publications: one on workplace culture and HR, one for personal essays. Writing is how I test whether an idea survives being explained.

If I cannot say it plainly, I have not finished thinking it.

5

Where it points

People strategy, organization development, research

Roles where analysis and empathy are both part of the job, and where the work is making organizations better places to do good work.

The most useful people strategy usually starts with a better question, not a new programme.

Still going. The next chapter is open.
